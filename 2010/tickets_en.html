Tickets

Tickets for YAPC::Asia Tokyo 2010 can be purchased via eplus or paypal (if you're a speaker, not including lightning talks, or if you're a volunteer staff, you do not need to purchase tickets).

This year we have the following types of tickets:

  • 2 Day Ticket (10/15 and 10/16) - 4000 yen
  • 1 Day Ticket (10/15) - 3000 yen
  • 1 Day Ticket (10/16) - 3000 yen

With each ticket you have an option to purchase the conference T-shirt along with the ticket, which will give youa 30% discount on the T-shirt compared to buying it at the conference.

There's also an student discount. If you purchase a student discount ticket, please make sure to bring a valid student ID along with you on the day of conference.

The instructions for eplus is a bit confusing, so unless you have no way to pay by credit card, please use paypal.

Paypal

Follow these instructions to pay via paypal:.

  • Choose your ticket type, and do the usual paypal transaction
  • Keep the Order ID that will be issued upon purchase (it will be emailed too)
  • Use the information on the email to display the two dimensional barcode
  • Bring the printed page or bring a device that can clearly display your barcode so our scanners can scan your record to the conference
